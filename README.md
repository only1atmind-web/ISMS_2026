# FBTR Integrated Industrial Safety System

This repository is the index for the proposed Integrated Industrial Safety
System (IISS) documentation suite for the Fast Breeder Test Reactor (FBTR).
The suite is intended to provide a unified, auditable framework for phased
plant-wide implementation and continuing operational assurance, aligned with
applicable AERB safety codes, statutory factory requirements, and internal
quality-assurance governance.

## Requested disposition

1. Circulate Parts I–IV for station-wide technical review, then publish the
   approved, controlled revisions on the FBTR Knowledge Management System
   (KMS). KMS publication is intended to centralize configuration control,
   prevent use of superseded local forms, and provide an accessible reference
   for plant engineers, shift supervisors, and induction.
2. Send Parts V–XI and the supplementary JHA/PTW procedure for
   multidisciplinary technical evaluation and collaborative refinement.
3. Record review comments, their disposition, document owners, revision
   identifiers, and approval evidence through the applicable document-control
   process before any KMS release.

The proposed review groups are Operations & Chemical Control; Mechanical,
Electrical & Civil Maintenance; Quality Assurance & Industrial Safety
(QA&ISD); IGCAR Fire Services & Safety Cell; and Training & Regulatory
Compliance Sections. The applicable functional groups should confirm
participation and nominate reviewers through the station's established
governance process.

**Approval and publication remain pending.** This index records the requested
review and release path; it does not grant approval, certify regulatory
compliance, or establish that any manual has been uploaded. The controlled
volumes, local applicability checks, and authorized approvals must be supplied
and verified before implementation or KMS publication.
